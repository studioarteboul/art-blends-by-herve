Pricing placement for Hervé Teboul works

## Recommendation

The **Exhibitions & Collections** page is a CV-style record of shows, distinctions and collections. Adding a price range here can feel out of place because visitors read that page for credibility and biography, not purchase details.

If the goal is to give buyers a pricing expectation before they inquire, the natural homes are:

1. **Works page** — Update the existing "Available works and pricing are shared on request" closing section to include the range. This keeps the discovery and pricing information on the same page as the artwork.
2. **Contact page** — Add the range near the "Available works" links or the pricing-inquiry subject. This is where purchase intent is highest.
3. **Exhibitions & Collections page** — Add a discreet "For collectors" note at the bottom of the page, after the Collections block, so it does not interrupt the CV reading flow.

## Proposed plan

- Add the bilingual price range text to the chosen location(s).
- English: "Works range from $5,000 to $25,000 USD."
- French: "Les œuvres sont offertes entre 5 000 $ et 25 000 $ US."
- Keep the existing typography and spacing system (small label + short sentence) so it feels consistent with the rest of the site.
- If placed on the Exhibitions page, frame it as a collector note rather than a sales banner.
